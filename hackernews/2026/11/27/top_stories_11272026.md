<p class="sum one"><span class='en'>Researchers discovered 40 charred papyrus fragments at a temple in the ancient city of Thebes, and those fragments are changing understanding of a famous ancient Egyptian curse ritual.</span><span class='zh'>研究人员在古代底比斯的一座神庙中发现了 40 份烧焦的纸莎草碎片，这些碎片正在改变人们对一项著名古埃及诅咒仪式的认识。</span></p>
<p class="sum full"><span class='en'>Researchers discovered 40 charred papyrus fragments at a temple in the ancient city of Thebes, and those fragments are changing understanding of a famous ancient Egyptian curse ritual. The fragments indicate the ritual was likely far more widespread and commonplace than previously believed. This finding revises views about how frequently and broadly that ritual was practiced in ancient Egypt.</span><span class='zh'>研究人员在古代底比斯的一座神庙中发现了 40 份烧焦的纸莎草碎片，这些碎片正在改变人们对一项著名古埃及诅咒仪式的认识。碎片显示这一仪式可能比此前认为的更为普遍和平常。此项发现修正了该仪式在古埃及社会中实施频率和范围的认知。</span></p>
