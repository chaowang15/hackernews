<p class="sum one"><span class='en'>Shakespeare&#x27;s Globe worked with the South London studio Typeland to create Amifer Folio, a typeface based on ornamental editorial devices from Shakespeare&#x27;s First Folio.</span><span class='zh'>莎士比亚环球剧院与南伦敦字体工作室 Typeland 合作，设计了 Amifer Folio 字体，其灵感来自莎士比亚《第一对开本》中的装饰性编辑图案。</span></p>
<p class="sum full"><span class='en'>Shakespeare&#x27;s Globe worked with the South London studio Typeland to create Amifer Folio, a typeface based on ornamental editorial devices from Shakespeare&#x27;s First Folio. The typeface is being used on posters for the theatre&#x27;s summer season.</span><span class='zh'>莎士比亚环球剧院与南伦敦字体工作室 Typeland 合作，设计了 Amifer Folio 字体，其灵感来自莎士比亚《第一对开本》中的装饰性编辑图案。该项目纪念这部文集出版 400 周年，并借鉴其中的木刻插图以及荒野与驯化环境的主题。该字体将用于剧院夏季演出季的海报。</span></p>

<h3 class="st"><a href="https://declarative-dragging.github.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span class='en'>Declarative Dragging</span><span class='zh'>声明式拖拽</span></a><span class="dom">declarative-dragging.github.io</span></h3>
<p class="sum one"><span class='en'>Declarative dragging proposes that interface authors define which future model states an element may reach by dragging.</span><span class='zh'>“声明式拖拽”提出，界面作者可以通过指定元素拖动后允许到达的模型状态来定义拖拽行为。</span></p>
<p class="sum full"><span class='en'>Declarative dragging proposes that interface authors define which future model states an element may reach by dragging. This makes a diagram element draggable by describing its allowed outcomes rather than implementing dragging behavior directly. The work was named the Best Paper at UIST 2026.</span><span class='zh'>“声明式拖拽”提出，界面作者可以通过指定元素拖动后允许到达的模型状态来定义拖拽行为。这样，创建可拖动的图表元素不必直接实现拖拽逻辑，而是描述其允许的结果。该研究获得了 UIST 2026 最佳论文奖。</span></p>
